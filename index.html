Varshney Lab

Functional genomics in zebrafish — genome editing, high-throughput screening, and functional assessment of human disease variants. This page collects the open-source software from the lab.

Tools

Umax Guide Designer

sgRNA design for zebrafish base editing with the ABE-Umax and TCBE-Umax editor families. Pick the base you want to change and it returns every guide that places it inside a published editing window, with bystander edits and codon consequences worked out.

base editing sgRNA design runs in the browser

Danio pegRNA Designer

Prime editing guide design for zebrafish. Returns every protospacer that can reach your edit, sweeps PBS and RTT lengths as a scored matrix, and picks the PBS whose melting temperature matches embryos at 28.5 °C — which is far shorter than the conventional 13 nt. Includes PE3 and PE3b nicking guides and GRCz11 coordinate lookup.

prime editing pegRNA design GRCz11 runs in the browser

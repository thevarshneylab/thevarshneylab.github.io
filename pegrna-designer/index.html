Parameters

The PBS range starts at 6 nt because Tm-matched zebrafish pegRNAs land far shorter than the classic 13 nt.

Constraints

Tm-matched design picks the PBS whose hybrid with the nicked target melts near the incubation temperature instead of a fixed length. Ponnienselvan et al. used 6–7 nt in zebrafish embryos on this basis.

pegRNA assembly

tevopreQ1 by default, and editable. Worth running a plain pegRNA alongside it — epegRNAs have not always outperformed in zebrafish.

How candidates are scored

The score is a triage heuristic, not a trained efficiency prediction. It rewards a short nick-to-edit distance, a PBS Tm close to the incubation temperature, at least 7 nt of homology 3′ of the edit, and an edit that lands in the PAM or seed so the corrected allele cannot be re-nicked. It penalises a 3′ extension beginning with C, poly-T runs that terminate Pol III early, and long PBS–spacer duplexes. For a trained prediction, run a shortlist through PRIDICT or DeepPrime — neither was trained on zebrafish embryos, so treat both as guidance.

Off-target checking

This page does not search the genome. Copy a spacer and run it through CRISPOR or Cas-OFFinder against Danio rerio GRCz11/danRer11. Prime editing produces fewer off-target events than nuclease editing, but the nicking sgRNA in a PE3 design deserves the same check as the pegRNA spacer.
